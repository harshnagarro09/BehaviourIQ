The pitch · be upfront

What to say plainly, and what comes next

Honest caveats

  • The data is simulated. It shows the method works, not that it works on real customers.
  • Uplift is estimated: there is no control group in the data.
  • Chances and profit cover 14 days, not a year.
  • The model is a simple, explainable regression.

Next steps

  • Load real transaction, promotion and customer history with the same columns.
  • Pilot on one or two campaigns with a held-out control group.
  • Review the customer type rules with the business.
  • Repeat the held-out check on real campaigns.
