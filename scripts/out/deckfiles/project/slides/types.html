Part 1 · The customer types used everywhere

Five customer types, from behaviour alone

Buys Anyways

117

Buys our brand at full price anyway. A discount just gives margin away.

Deal-Only

106

Buys mostly when discounted and responds to almost any promotion.

Stock-Up

79

Buys 2 to 4 times the usual quantity on promotion, then holds off for weeks.

Switcher

109

Normally buys a competitor and tries us when the discount is deep.

Ignores

109

Rarely buys our brand and barely reacts to promotions.

Types are assigned from observed behaviour with simple rules. There is no type label in the data.
